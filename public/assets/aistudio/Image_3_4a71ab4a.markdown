## Brand & Style

This design system defines the biocultural oceanic eco-brutalist digital presence for architectural discourse, computational parametric tools, and studio commerce. The philosophy fuses marine ecology, mineral concrete textures, and hyper-structured computational geometry. 

Targeted at computational architects, speculative spatial designers, environmental engineers, and institutional collectors, the interface conveys rigorous tectonic precision, monolithic mass, and raw oceanic depth. The aesthetic rejects decorative softness in favor of deliberate architectural grid lines, high-contrast borders, raw data telemetry, and physical spatial presence reminiscent of maritime technical documents and offshore concrete monoliths.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is derived directly from benthic and abyssal marine surveys combined with pale glacial surface planes:

- **Primary (`#033141` — Deep Marine Dark / Abyssal Teal):** The foundational anchor for structural tectonic borders, primary typography, high-priority interactive states, and solid monospaced badges.
- **Secondary (`#194C60` — Ocean Slate):** Utilized for structural sub-divisions, secondary interactive controls, blueprint grids, and computational metadata tiers.
- **Tertiary (`#4A798A` — Muted Glacial Teal):** Acts as a low-contrast delineator, active tab highlights, hover states, and muted schematic annotations.
- **Accent Mist (`#A9CAD3` — Soft Mist Blue):** Employed for interactive hover fills, secondary selection tokens, and telemetry indicators.
- **Neutral Light (`#C6E2ED` — Pale Ice Surface):** The ambient architectural background surface tint, evoking frozen ocean mist and cast mineral silica.
- **Surface Crisp (`#F4F8F9` — Bleached Concrete):** Used for elevated computational cards, product canvases, and contrast reading panels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements a high-tension tripartite dialectic:
1. **Syne (Display & Headlines):** Imparts avant-garde sculptural geometry and heavy tectonic mass. Used in uppercase or tight tracking for titles, brandmarks, and monolithic project names.
2. **Cormorant (Editorial Accents & Philosophical Inscriptions):** Imparts biological delicacy and humanistic inquiry to contrast heavy engineering. Reserved for curated project descriptions, spatial manifestos, and biocultural questions.
3. **Space Mono (Labels, Metadata, & Telemetry):** Anchors the technical computational aspect of the store. Encodes unit metrics, geospatial coordinates, polygon counts, script parameters, and technical specs in full uppercase with expanded tracking.
4. **DM Sans (Body & Operational Prose):** Provides high legibility for dense technical documentation, licensing agreements, and structural analysis.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a modular architectural coordinate system:
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column rigid framework on desktop screens (>1024px) utilizing 24px gutters and 48px outer boundary margins. Tablets (768px - 1023px) compress to an 8-column grid with 16px gutters. Mobile viewports (<768px) resolve to a 4-column column layout with 12px gutters and 16px margins.
- **Rhythm & Alignment:** Spacing strictly adheres to 8px base units. All modules and data containers span explicit column units (e.g., 3-col technical metadata, 9-col computational viewport).
- **Brutalist Cadence:** Sections are separated by continuous 2px horizontal datum lines rather than empty vertical gaps, reinforcing physical floorplate and blueprint conventions.

## Elevation & Depth

True to architectural brutalism, this design system rejects standard drop shadows, diffused lighting, and artificial ambient blurs. Depth is conveyed strictly through structural construction:

- **Tectonic Linearity:** Spatial depth is defined by 2px solid structural borders in `#033141` or `#194C60`.
- **Concrete Stacking:** Layering relies on hard surface offsets. Active layers and floating parameter modals utilize hard, zero-blur architectural drop-shifts (e.g., `4px 4px 0px #033141` or `6px 6px 0px #194C60`).
- **Surface Tiering:** Base canvas operates on `#F4F8F9` or `#C6E2ED`, while nested computational panels sit within recessed `#A9CAD3` / `#194C60` containers with opaque high-contrast contrast lines.

## Shapes

The geometry is zero-radius (`0` / Sharp). Every container, button, chip, thumbnail, and input field is strictly rectilinear. Form edges are cut with 90-degree orthogonal precision to reflect cast concrete panels, architectural floor plans, and computational raw geometry. Chamfers (45-degree corner notches) are optionally allowed via CSS clip-path for technical spec tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#033141` background, sharp 0px radius, 2px solid `#033141` border, uppercase `Space Mono` typography in `#F4F8F9`. Hover state shifts background to `#194C60` with a 4px 4px offset box border.
- **Secondary / Studio Utility:** Transparent background, 2px solid `#033141` border, `#033141` text. Hover state inverts background to `#C6E2ED`.
- **Technical Action / Download:** Heavy brutalist button with split layout: left side contains parametric script name in `Syne`, right side contains file size and format in `Space Mono` divided by a 2px vertical internal keyline.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- Constructed with a 1px or 2px solid `#033141` border, 0px border radius, padding of `0.25rem 0.5rem`.
- Text styled in `label-spec` (`Space Mono`, uppercase).
- Category chips utilize alternating backgrounds: `#C6E2ED` (Marine Spec), `#A9CAD3` (Computational Asset), and `#033141` with white text for active filters.

### Form Inputs & Telemetry Fields
- Rectangular 2px solid `#033141` borders on crisp `#F4F8F9` background.
- Floating or prefixed coordinate labels (e.g., `[PARAM_01]`) styled in `Space Mono` muted `#4A798A`.
- Focus state eliminates soft glow rings, replacing them with an immediate inverse stroke change to `#194C60` and a 2px offset outline.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp square boxes (0px radius) framed in 2px solid `#033141`.
- Checked status renders a solid inner square block (`#033141`) or an architectural diagonal hatch pattern rather than rounded checkmarks.

### Cards & Computational Product Tiles
- Enclosed in continuous 2px solid `#033141` perimeter frames.
- Internal layout is partitioned by 1px or 2px coordinate divider lines separating asset renders, parametric spec tables (polys, filetype, biomaterial source), and commerce interactions.
- Hover interaction triggers an immediate structural 4px 4px `#033141` hard shadow shift.

### Architectural Viewport & Blueprint Frames
- Technical canvas wrappers flanked by crosshair corners (`+`) and scale ruler markings along x/y borders.
- Monospaced coordinate status overlays positioned in the top-right corner to show model parameters and environmental metrics.
